# 📱 Offline TikTok-Style Video Player (Focus Mode)

A 100% offline, privacy-first, minimalist TikTok/Reels-style vertical video player focused entirely on pure video playback.

---

## ✨ Key Features

1. **▶️ Center Play / Pause Button**:
   - Clean frosted glass Play/Pause button right in the **center of the video**.
   - Tap the video or click the center button to play/pause instantly.
   - Auto-hides with the video controls for pure immersion.

2. **📺 Lower Right Fullscreen Button**:
   - Fullscreen button moved to the **lower right corner** (above scrubber) for convenient thumb / mouse reach.
   - Smooth auto-framing on both portrait and landscape devices.

3. **🔄 Lower Left Rotate Video Button**:
   - Rotate button in the **lower left corner**.
   - Click to rotate video by 90° (`0° → 90° → 180° → 270° → 0°`).
   - Or press **`R`** on keyboard.

4. **🔂 Auto-Next & Loop Toggle**:
   - Toggle between **Auto-Next** (automatically transitions to next video when current video ends) and **Loop 1** (repeats single video continuously).
   - Dedicated round pill toggle button in the top bar.
   - Or press **`L`** on keyboard.

5. **🎯 Perfect Round Counter & Buttons**:
   - Clean circular 38x38 icon buttons.
   - Dedicated round pill video counter (`1 / 3`) with crisp typography and spacing.

6. **⏩ Custom Playback Speeds**:
   - Dedicated speed menu with **1.0x, 1.5x, 1.75x, 2.0x, 2.5x, 3.0x, 3.5x, 4.0x**.
   - Tap the speed badge (e.g. `1.0x`) to choose any speed instantly.
   - Or press **`S`** on keyboard.

7. **📂 Always Load Folder (All Videos at Once)**:
   - Clicking **"Folder"** opens your device's folder picker.
   - Automatically scans and loads **every video inside that folder** (sorted numerically & alphabetically) without selecting files one by one.
   - Also supports dragging and dropping a whole video folder onto the window.

8. **🤏 2-Finger Pinch Zoom & Pan**:
   - Pinch with 2 fingers to zoom in (up to 4.0x) and zoom out.
   - Pan around with 1 finger when zoomed in.
   - On PC: `Ctrl + Mouse Wheel` to zoom, double-click to toggle 2.0x zoom, or press `0` to reset.

9. **👁️ Auto-Hiding Controls (Pure Video Immersion)**:
   - All controls smoothly vanish after 2.8 seconds of inactivity.
   - Tap the screen, move the mouse, or press any key to bring them back.

## ⌨️ Desktop Keyboard Shortcuts

| Key | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`↓` (Down Arrow) / `J`** | Next Video |
| **`↑` (Up Arrow) / `K`** | Previous Video |
| **`Space`** or **Click** | Instant Play / Pause |
| **`R`** | Rotate Video 90° |
| **`L`** | Toggle Auto-Next vs Loop 1 |
| **`S`** | Cycle Speed (1.0x, 1.5x, 1.75x, 2.0x, 2.5x, 3.0x, 3.5x, 4.0x) |
| **`M`** | Mute / Unmute Audio |
| **`F`** | Fullscreen Toggle |
| **`Ctrl + Wheel`** | Zoom In / Out |
| **`0` (Zero)** | Reset Zoom to 1.0x |
| **`→` / `←`** | Seek Forward / Backward 5s |
| **Mouse Wheel** | Scroll smoothly between videos |
